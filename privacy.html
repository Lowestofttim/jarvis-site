Jarvis

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who this applies to

Jarvis is a private household assistant used only by the family that runs it. The only person who grants it access to a Google account is that account's owner. It is not offered to the public.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): upcoming events, so Jarvis can answer questions about the owner's schedule and show busy times.
  • Gmail (read-only): recent message headers and short previews, so Jarvis can summarise and answer questions about the owner's mail. Jarvis cannot send, delete or change email.

Jarvis asks only for read-only permissions and uses them only when the owner asks it to.

Where data is kept

Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer at home, in folders that only the owner's account can read. Backups are encrypted and kept on the owner's own drives. Jarvis has no servers of its own and runs no analytics or tracking.

To answer a question, the relevant text may be sent to the AI model service the owner has chosen for their own assistant. That service processes it under the owner's own account and terms.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else and not used to train AI models. A screen in the home shows only times and counts, never email or event contents.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it, and the owner can delete it at any time. Deleted items leave encrypted backups as those backups expire. The owner can withdraw Jarvis's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
