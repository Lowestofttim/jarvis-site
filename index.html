Jarvis

Jarvis is a private household assistant run by one family on their own computer. It is not a product or a public service, and it has no other users.

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail (read-only) so it can answer questions such as "what's on today?" and show simple summaries, like busy times and message counts, on a screen at home.
